FAQ

Before we start.

Do you need access to our SaaS product?+

Usually, yes. Product access or a clean walkthrough helps us capture accurate workflows and understand which product moments deserve focus. A rough screen recording can also work for early planning.

Can MBA Studio handle the script and storyboard?+

Yes. We can work from a finished script, refine an existing draft, or develop the message and storyboard as part of a larger product-video scope.

Can one video become social content too?+

Yes. Repurposing should be planned before production. We can structure the master video so it can be adapted into shorter feature clips, vertical cutdowns and other campaign assets.

Are revisions included?+

Review rounds are built into the production workflow. Major changes to an already approved script, voiceover or direction may require additional production scope.

Do you support multilingual versions?+

Yes. Existing productions can be localized with translated scripts, voiceovers, captions and adjusted on-screen text where required.
